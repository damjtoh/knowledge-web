# 04 — Remove a saved offline copy

**What to build:** A reader can remove the Web Projection's saved content from a device and choose whether to save it again later.

**Blocked by:** 02 — Save the entire Web Projection offline.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A visible Remove offline copy action clears this projection's saved worker and cached publication without affecting other origins or Web Projections.
- [ ] The reader no longer displays Ready offline after removal or after browser storage is cleared.
- [ ] Later online visits do not silently resume whole-site saving; the reader can choose Save for offline use again.
- [ ] The existing focused browser journey verifies removal and the resulting displayed state, without another full reader build.
